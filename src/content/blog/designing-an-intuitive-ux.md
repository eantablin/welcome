Generally you want to maximize your returning users, and you do that by making
their experience as smooth and pleasant as possible. Here are a couple of tips
to help you maximize retention.

**Assume users are idiots.** The simplest way to build around the inevitable
headaches they'll run into is to make everything as easy and straightforward
as possible. Stick to a clean UI that prevents them from getting lost.

**Repetition makes the dream work.** Repeat common patterns so they get
ingrained, making the experience more intuitive — the less users have to think
about what they're doing, the more time they'll spend on your content. Think
about how you'd want to traverse a large website: it's nice to know it's
organized in a standard way, whether via a hamburger menu or an always-present
side panel. However you choose to organize, stick with your patterns.
